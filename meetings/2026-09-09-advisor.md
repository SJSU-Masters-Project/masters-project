## 2) Accomplished During This Meeting

- Uday presented the extraction findings: skill linkers drop phrases that fall below a similarity cut-off, published work measures this loss only for text spans, and skills a candidate shows without naming are inferred only for job descriptions.
- Himanshu presented the matching and fairness findings: the strongest matchers produce ranked lists, allocation methods take scores as given, and fairness audits examine only the final scores or selections.
- Agreed on the gap the project will address: no system connects extraction, matching, allocation and auditing, so no one checks whether the way skills are extracted changes who gets matched.
- The advisor suggested writing this as a formal problem statement with the state of the art, the gap, the proposed solution and the deliverables, and tracking all tasks on a Jira board.
- Discussed datasets. The Djinni recruitment dataset (real anonymized resumes and job descriptions, MIT licence) fits as the main corpus, and the TalentCLEF 2026 job-person matching task can serve as an external benchmark.

## 3) Issues / Blockers

- The Djinni dataset has no hiring outcomes, so relevance labels will have to be created by the team.
